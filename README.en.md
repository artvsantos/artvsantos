## About me

I'm a **Systems Analyst and Developer** with a degree from **UNOPAR**, focused on **back-end**.

I work with **Python and Django** on the part nobody sees: data modeling, business rules and routes the front end can consume without pain. I also write **JavaScript** day to day, and I like closing the loop with **automated tests** — Pytest for the logic, Selenium for what only breaks in the browser.

I'm looking for a **junior back-end developer** role, building solutions that are organized and maintainable by whoever comes next.
